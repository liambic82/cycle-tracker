## Account eligibility before submission

Google's current [Play Console requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10788890?hl=en) require organization accounts for health apps. Its [health categories](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13996367) include wellness and sleep tracking, so Trifelle's non-medical disclaimer does not establish an exemption. The reported personal account is therefore an unresolved submission requirement; sharing Worthward's publisher name does not resolve it.

Google documents [converting the existing personal Play account](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16260648?hl=en) using a verified organization website, organization payments profile, D-U-N-S number and identity/contact verification. This offers a route to preserve the shared account. Confirm Plowden Digital's legal organization details and the actual Console flow with the owner before changing account or payments settings; these affect Worthward too. The guidance says to wait at least 72 hours after conversion before submitting new apps. No conversion, new account, purchase or support request has been performed. Development and local testing can continue.

Next account step: establish what verifiable business identity the owner can use and confirm the appropriate Google verification route. This plan does not assume that a particular legal structure, such as an LLC, is required. Account conversion and any registration/payment decisions need a concrete owner decision after those requirements are clear.

## Release sequence

1. **Complete reliability and privacy hardening.** The first slice is implemented: Android ciphertext files with verified pointer commits, interrupted-write/deletion tests, export-cache cleanup and removal of unused broad storage/overlay permissions. Finish SDK/network inventory, recovery/accessibility review and the consolidated phone checks. See [architecture](architecture.md#android-storage-and-export-hardening-0230) and [validation](validation.md).
2. **Prepare the store package and disclosures.** Create a production AAB using the store identity and a dedicated upload key, validate App Signing arrangements and preserve credentials outside Git. Publish a Trifelle-specific privacy policy, expose it in the app, and prepare the Data safety, Health apps and Health Connect declarations from verified app/SDK behavior. Complete listing copy, screenshots, content rating, audience, support details, reviewer access and pricing.
3. **Run an internal Play test.** Verify the actual Play-delivered build, production screenshot protection, sleep permission access, notifications, offline behavior and backup transfer from the existing preview. Review the Play pre-launch report. The local APK is not evidence that a Play-distributed bundle works.
4. **Run closed validation and satisfy the Console access requirements.** The reported new personal account is subject to **12 testers continuously opted in for 14 days**, followed by a production-access application. Account conversion may change which gate applies; confirm Trifelle's dashboard after resolving the account requirement above rather than treating 12/14 as the only release dependency. Keep actual feature-use, feedback and fix records either way. No Trifelle closed test has begun; Worthward's test/access request does not establish Trifelle completion. [Google's testing requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en), checked October 30, 2026.
5. **Review the release candidate.** Resolve material failures, verify final declarations/assets and obtain the owner's release decision before publishing. A closed-test milestone is not a 1.0 or production-release decision.

## Preview-to-Play transfer and test evidence

The preview and store package IDs differ. A Play installation will have a separate journal; it cannot automatically read the preview's private files. First export an encrypted backup from the preview, retain that app, install the Play test build, then restore and compare entries, attachments and settings. Biometric unlock and reminder/automatic-import opt-ins must be enabled again. Do not delete the preview or its backup until transfer is verified.

For each test record, capture app version/code, device/OS, installation source, checklist item, result and a minimal reproduction. Prefer fictional records for failures, screenshots and Play review. Never commit real health records, backup files, passphrases, keys or tester account details. Keep Play opt-in dates separate from reported functional passes and automated checks.
