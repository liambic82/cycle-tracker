## Full description

Keep your cycle history and daily observations together in a personal journal.

Trifelle helps you record periods, flow, symptoms, moods, medications and sleep, then review what you have logged over time. Add a past period with its start and finish, pin commonly used symptoms, and arrange daily sections to suit you.

Your journal is encrypted on your device. There is no Trifelle online account or cloud sync in this version. Use a passphrase to open it, with optional biometric unlock on supported phones. Make encrypted backups to keep a separate copy or move your journal to another installation.

- Review recorded history in the calendar and charts.
- Record your existing medication schedule and doses, with optional local reminders.
- Keep medical notes, appointments, lab records and selected attachments together.
- Import sleep shared with Android Health Connect by compatible source apps. Review selected nights or separately enable import from a chosen source when you unlock. Closed-app sleep syncing is not included.
- Create a doctor summary from the dates and sections you choose. CSV, PDF and exported attachments are readable files; review their contents before sharing.
- Choose from the included palettes and backgrounds, or use a selected photo.

Reminders require notification permission and are subject to Android's delivery and battery controls. Wearable import requires a source app that supplies sleep records to Health Connect; Trifelle does not connect directly to your wearable account.

Keep your passphrase and a separate backup. There is no password-reset or journal-recovery service. Deleting local app data does not delete exported files or records in other apps.

Trifelle is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Period predictions are optional and experimental, and must not be used as contraception guidance. Educational hormone curves do not measure your hormone levels.
