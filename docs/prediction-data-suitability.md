# New prediction data: suitability and access review

October 11, 2026, after completion of the Zenodo comparisons. **No newly reviewed source is accepted for full-policy or prospective validation.** This is a targeted review of the candidates below, not a claim that no suitable dataset exists. Only public documentation was examined; no new participant files were acquired, agreements accepted, or providers contacted.

## Requirements for the next evaluation

A useful source needs documented reuse for this project; participant separation from examined records; enough preceding history and follow-up; explicit onset labels and observation gaps; context/completeness information known at issuance; and entry/correction timestamps or an equivalent immutable input history. The [prospective protocol](prediction-prospective-protocol.md) describes the minimum records. A study's observation date does not automatically tell us when the predictor learned that fact. Missing metadata must remain unknown.

For the current policy, six prior intervals means seven starts before an offered forecast is possible. That requirement must fit within the policy's 365-day history window. Short diary blocks cannot be stitched across unobserved years to satisfy it. Availability and withholding can still be studied when forecasts are unavailable, but that does not establish forecast accuracy.

## Intake questions for a prospective provider

Before accepting records, obtain a source-specific answer and supporting document for each item:

1. What use is permitted: this paid-app research, model development/evaluation, derived artifacts, reports and any later product incorporation? Who may access the data, in what environment, and under what retention terms?
2. Are participant IDs stable across visits, extracts and studies, and can overlap with previously examined cohorts be assessed without identifying individuals?
3. Are onset dates, entry timestamps, edits/deletions and original values retained? Which times represent the event, the user's report, import or server receipt? What time-zone/clock limitations apply?
4. How is onset distinguished from spotting? Are observation windows, explicit negative reports, missing diary days, withdrawal and uncertain/corrected labels available?
5. Are context changes, relevant treatment/life-stage information and completeness known contemporaneously? Are labels self-reported, adjudicated or inferred, and by whom?
6. How much pre-issue history and subsequent follow-up are available per person? Can suitability be assessed from a codebook and aggregate counts before confirmatory outcomes are opened?
7. What recruitment and exclusion rules selected the cohort, and which intended users are poorly represented? Can target labels be assessed without seeing predictions, to reduce outcome-assessment bias?

These are intake questions, not a message already sent to any provider. The project owner may choose a provider contact later; no external outreach or expense has been initiated.

## Recommended path

The [internal statistical review](prediction-statistical-review-2026-10-12.md) is complete; the owner declined external review on October 12. If a future study is chosen, assess either an authorized provider extract that meets the data requirements or a separately consented prospective collection. The latter needs an instrument that preserves issued forecasts and as-known inputs; ordinary app backups are insufficient. Establish a feasible sample and appropriate stewardship before recruitment. Continue ordinary development with proportionate experimental prediction claims; external review is not a project gate.
