## Coverage of the original brief

The additions refine the brief rather than replace it. The remaining scope includes:

- **Calendar and history:** explicit missed/skipped-cycle markers, days since the last period, setup-time historical entry, optional clots/flooding, product logging, and import research. Back-filling individual days already works.
- **Predictions:** distinct typical and personal estimates, minimum history, ranges/confidence, recent-cycle weighting, outlier flags, and optional temperature/LH/mucus/pregnancy-test inputs. No prediction engine is implemented.
- **Symptoms:** all four reference lists, separate access to uncommon symptoms, custom tags, severity for additional symptoms, cramp-severity trends, and optional sleep, pain-location, hot-flash, mood/energy, activity, stress, weight, blood-pressure, and sexual-health records. Existing custom symptom labels do not cover every structured field.
- **Medications:** names/doses/times, on/off cycles, pack/placebo schedules, taken/skipped/late records, as-needed doses and reasons, supplements, optional reminders, patch/ring/injection/refill reminders, titration, side-effect notes, and hormonal-medication context.
- **Medical records:** draw date and cycle-day context, results with units and lab reference ranges, calendar presentation, photo/PDF attachments, appointments, doctor questions, diagnoses, procedures, and allergies.
- **Daily context:** due medications, educational phase/hormone information, personal lab overlays, and a later home-screen widget. Recorded cycle day already appears in the daily editor.
- **Doctor reports:** selectable sections and dates, cycle/bleeding statistics, notable bleeding, symptoms/severity, medications/doses/adherence, and later lab results. Structured clot data depends on its logging model. Optional doctor-discussion prompts need reviewed clinical criteria before implementation.
- **Notifications:** individual opt-ins and timing controls for medications, daily logging, and later prediction-dependent alerts; discreet lock-screen text. No notifications are implemented yet.
- **Privacy and customization:** local encrypted storage, passphrase/optional biometric lock, backup/restore, and CSV are implemented. Optional end-to-end encrypted sync, theme/dark-mode choices, and full accessibility validation remain planned. The brief's PIN-or-biometric request does not imply that a separate PIN flow exists.

Medical statements and diagnostic labels in the source and example apps are design inputs, not validated product copy. Before phase, hormone, ovulation, or warning features ship, review their factual basis, irregular-cycle behavior, and medication limitations. Keep measured records, user observations, and estimates visibly distinct.

## Development order following this review

1. Complete the symptom catalog and improve selection, including optional perimenopause visibility and preservation of existing/custom labels and backups.
2. Improve recorded-history charts and year/month/day navigation. This provides the requested visual context using logged data before adding estimates.
3. Add optional product and sexual-health records, then medication schedules/dose logging and a configurable doctor PDF. Keep export and backup formats complete as the model grows.
4. Build carefully labeled predictions and reviewed everyday education with medication context, then richer lab overlays and customization.

This is a revised development sequence, not a promise that every item ships in the next APK. Store scope and exact pricing remain open. No ads, partner sharing, premium purchase flow, or cloud service are introduced by this review.
