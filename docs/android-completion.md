## Explicit remaining scope

- **Device test pass:** implemented features are not proven on real hardware until the owner runs the consolidated checklist. Keep the last reported phone pass (0.15.0) distinct from current automated/build evidence. Native iOS development follows Android validation.
- **Prediction research:** the [evidence review and synthetic evaluation](prediction-research-2026-10-11.md), [benchmark/intake](prediction-benchmark.md), [record preparation](prediction-record-preparation.md), [controlled development comparison](prediction-controlled-comparisons.md), [held-out evaluations](prediction-heldout-results.md) and [internal statistical review](prediction-statistical-review-2026-10-12.md) are complete. The weighted candidate did not improve the primary metric in any group. Full-policy accuracy on the intended population remains unknown; retain the experimental median and proportionate wording. Personal phase/ovulation estimates, calibrated probabilities, typical hormone-curve overlays and automated clinical discussion prompts are not implemented or validated. More input fields do not establish a valid prediction method. The new cycle observations withhold the period estimate when applicable.
- **Optional future evaluation:** the [protocol draft](prediction-prospective-protocol.md) and [candidate-data assessment](prediction-data-suitability.md) preserve planning for a possible study. Suitable authorized records, study size/acceptance criteria and a separately designed local audit feature would need resolution if chosen. These are research questions, not external-review dependencies or blockers to ordinary Android development. No new Android collection or study has started.
- **Other-app import mappings:** the new importer supports its documented template, not arbitrary vendor exports. Actual export samples are needed to build and test mappings. Encrypted backup remains the full-fidelity transfer path.
- **Medication reconciliation:** past plan corrections now have an explicit interval/record-count review. Recorded doses retain their original snapshots; users correct actual dose facts separately. Automatic rewriting or inference of taken/missed doses is intentionally absent.
- **Wearable compatibility:** the connector can read other Health Connect sleep sources but each needs hardware validation. Source deletion does not silently erase imported copies. Automatic reconciliation and background syncing remain later work.
- **Visual refinement:** approved customization is implemented. Further layout changes require a concrete approved proposal, respecting the owner's clean, feminine direction.
- **Release preparation:** security/accessibility checks, internal clinical-copy/source checks, final public privacy/contact information, Play declarations/review, store metadata/pricing and a separate release-scope decision remain. These are not established by an APK build.
- **Deferred by the owner:** hosted encrypted sync and automatic/background wearable syncing. iOS/HealthKit work follows the Android pass.

Do not describe 0.18.1 as “all Android work complete” while the device and release items remain. Optional future research is separate. This file keeps the Android-first objective and the remaining dependencies visible across development sessions.
